PLAYER’S FIELD GUIDE

Your table, in one place.

Choose your Circle, then your character. Click an action to load its rating and gilded die.

Build your pool

Spend drive for extra dice. The app deducts those points when you roll. Add bonus dice for help or an ability and adjust any related costs on the sheet. Click the numbered pool dice to choose which are gilded before rolling. A Stamina Training die spends one of your Circle’s shared gilded dice.

Choose the result

The normal result is selected for you. You can choose a gilded alternative, then accept the result. Accepting a gilded result recovers one corresponding drive, up to its maximum, once for that roll.

Burn resistance

Resistance rerolls the action dice and keeps bonus dice. The app deducts one matching resistance. Use the newest resistance result to reroll again. You can always make a fresh action roll using the Roll button.

Keep the sheet current

Use the small + and − buttons for drives, resistance, marks, and Circle resources. Edit sheet changes ratings, gilded actions, ability choices, and your dossier. Scars and the narrative effects of abilities are recorded manually with your GM.

Articles

Read Keith’s and Wysel’s Canva articles in the shared archive. Filter by author, open an article, and use the zoom controls to read its columns. To add one, upload its PNG or JPG to the repository’s assets/articles folder, then enter its image path in Add article. Titles, publication names, and dates are optional. Articles remain saved when you clear the table log.

Your Circle

The Circle sheet tracks illumination, level, and downtime resources. At 24 illumination it gains a level and resets the track. Choose a new Circle ability, then each character chooses two different advancement options. All characters begin at level 2, and ability choices can come from any class. Choose abilities also lets you correct abilities already owned. Export backup saves a copy of your Circle, including the latest 100 log entries. Everyone at this trusted table can edit sheets. The Lightkeeper seat is a view for the GM.
